Level 1: The Park · v0.4 preview: Easy mode and Real Shoot mode

  • Ride. Your cart rolls along the park path by itself. Tap Stop to wait quietly, Go to roll on.
  • Look. Drag anywhere to look around. Use the zoom slider (or pinch) to get close.
  • Listen first. When a bird calls, tap Listen and name it. Headphones help: the sound comes from the bird's side.
  • Snap. Put the bird in the white frame, hold still, tap SNAP.
  • A 2-star photo of all 5 park birds finishes the level.
  • Photo School. Four short lessons you pass by taking a real photo: Framing, Timing, Light, then Focus. Tap the lesson chip at the top any time.
  • Two modes. Easy is point and shoot. Real Shoot gives you a real camera: shutter speed, aperture, ISO and manual focus, unlocked one at a time. Tap the birds chip (top left) to switch.

PC: drag the mouse to look, wheel to zoom, Space to snap, S stop/go, L listen, J journal, F focus (once unlocked).
